Questions answered upfront

The things everyone asks.

Do I actually own it?

Yes. The site, the files, the domain and every account are yours. If you want to move it somewhere else next year you can, and we hand over everything you need to do it.

Do I have to pay every month?

Not for the website. The $700 covers the build and the first year of hosting. After that year, hosting and the domain renewal are the only running costs and they are small. We tell you the exact figures before you pay anything.

What if I do not like it?

Then you do not pay. We build it first on purpose. If the finished thing is not right for you, you walk away and you owe us nothing.

How do my reviews get on the site?

Straight from your Google listing, word for word, with the reviewer name as it appears. Nothing is written or invented by us. If your rating is 4.6, the site says 4.6.

I already have a Facebook page. Is that not enough?

It helps, but it does not do the same job. A Facebook page is hard to find on Google, it buries your phone number, and it puts your competitors in the sidebar next to your own page.

What does the automation actually do?

When you miss a call, the caller gets a text back inside a minute saying you will call them straight back. It also handles booking confirmations, reminders and follow-ups. Most owners find out what a missed call was worth only after they stop missing them.

Which businesses do you work with?

Owner-run service businesses. Most of our work is towing and roadside recovery, appliance repair, mobile mechanics, electricians, plumbers, HVAC, landscaping, locksmiths and cleaning. If you run the jobs yourself and the phone is how customers reach you, you are the right fit.

Who are you?

VexelTech Solutions, a Vexel Scales LLC brand, based in Richmond, Texas. We build websites, branding, marketing and automation for owner-run service businesses. We work remotely, so where you are does not change what you get. Call the number on this page and you get a person.
